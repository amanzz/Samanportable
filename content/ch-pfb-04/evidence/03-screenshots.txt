# Artefact 3 - screenshots, preview against the porta-cabins design lock

Captured with puppeteer-core against system Chrome at desktop 1440 and mobile 390.
Structural counts are taken from the DOM, not from the stitched image, because
CLAUDE.md records that fullPage tiling repaints sticky chrome and that lazy images
can capture blank. Every content image was forced eager and decoded first.

each they are the tiled captures CLAUDE.md warns are unreliable evidence, and the DOM
counts below are what they were taken for. The single-viewport shots kept here are
