READING
-------
CH-02 scores 74 against the design-lock hub's 78 on the same build, and well
above the untouched sibling page's 60. Its CLS is 0.015: every image on the page
carries explicit width and height, which artefact 5 asserts separately.

The live design lock scores 65 over the public internet. That number is not
comparable to the three local ones - different network, different host, a CDN and
third-party tags - and is included only because the build prompt asks for the
comparison against the live page. The like-for-like comparison is the local pair.

No page-level performance regression: CH-02 is the fastest of the two local
container-house pages measured and is within a few points of the design lock.
