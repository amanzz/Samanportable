ARTEFACT 10 - THE FIVE DESCRIPTION-TAB FRAMES ACTUALLY LOAD AND PAINT

Ruling 3 puts five 16:9 frames INSIDE the Description tab. They are emitted as bare
inline <img> in descriptionHtml, exactly as the porta-cabins design lock publishes its
own, and the shared OptimizedContent layer adds its standard classes and loading=lazy.

Because they are lazy, a naive probe that scrolls the page once and then measures
reads them as 0x0 with complete=false - they have not fetched yet. That is a
MEASUREMENT artefact, not a page defect, and it is also why the first full-page
screenshot pass captured them blank. scripts/po06-screenshots.js now forces every
image eager and awaits decode() before the shutter; the desktop capture grew from
10,307 px to 13,307 px tall once the five frames painted.
