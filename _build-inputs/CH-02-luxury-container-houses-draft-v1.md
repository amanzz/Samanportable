#### Is this a design gallery or a product you can order

Search for a luxury container house in India and the results split in two. Half of them are inspiration boards and architecture features from other countries, showing one-off houses built by architects on site. The other half are listings from manufacturers. This page is the second kind. Everything shown here is a factory-built product with a published size, a published room and opening schedule, and a published price, made in SAMAN's own facilities at Bangalore and Greater Noida and delivered as a finished unit.

That distinction matters more than it sounds. A container house built on site by a contractor is priced after design; a factory build is priced before it. The six sizes on this page were drawn first and costed second, which is why every one of them carries a ladder you can read without a meeting. What you cannot do here is buy a one-off. If your brief needs a shape that is not one of these six, it becomes a project quotation with its own engineering, not a catalogue price.

It is also worth being clear about the word luxury, because the market uses it loosely. On this page it refers to a defined specification level: an upgraded interior substrate, a full wet core, engineered support around large glazing, and a controlled finish schedule. It does not mean a different steel platform from the rest of the range, and it does not mean imported fittings. Where the specification stops, the page says so.

#### What makes a container house a luxury build

Three lines in the structural schedule are specific to this build rather than shared with the standard range. The floor frame is a premium residential grid, roughly 100 x 50 x 3 mm primary and 80 x 40 x 3 mm secondary members, locally strengthened under stone and tile, premium cabinetry, wet rooms, wide openings and joined-villa lines. It is designed for deflection as well as strength, because a rigid finish stops being serviceable long before a floor is structurally in trouble. A heavy island, a tub or a safe needs a marked support zone.

The second is the opening structure. Corner posts of the 60 x 60 x 3 mm class are joined by engineered posts, lintels and sill beams around full-height glazing, sliders, cantilevered features and multi-module openings, and the unit keeps its transport stiffness before it is joined on site. Removable transport braces and the final connection frames are identified on the erection sequence rather than improvised.

The third is the roof frame: 60 x 40 x 2.5 mm class rafters and 50 x 50 x 2.5 mm purlins, with dedicated supports for cove ceilings, concealed ducts, sunshades and approved canopy interfaces. Decorative ceiling and lighting loads are carried by secondary framing, never by the roof sheet. Roof terraces, solar, pergolas and water tanks are separate structural packages and are quoted as such.

Everything visible follows from those three. Floors are 19 mm marine or BWP plywood, or 18 mm cement-fibre board, on a verified close-spaced frame, with a set-down, waterproof backer and threshold containment in wet rooms. Walls are lined in 12 mm moisture-resistant plywood or board behind premium laminate, HPL, gypsum, acoustic panels or wood-effect surfaces over an accessible service frame. Ceilings are 12.5 mm gypsum or premium laminated with cove and recessed lighting zones and access panels. The walking surface is 5 to 6 mm premium SPC or LVT in dry rooms and anti-skid tile in the bath and kitchen.

#### Is a container house legal in India, and on what land

A container house is treated as a structure, not as a vehicle, so the approvals that apply are the ones that already apply to building on your land: the local municipal or panchayat building bylaws, setbacks, ground coverage, and any zoning restriction on the plot. A factory-built unit does not change what is permitted; it changes how the structure is delivered. On agricultural land in particular, the rules on permanent construction vary widely by state, and buyers should confirm the position for their own plot before ordering.

SAMAN supplies the unit. #### Warranty, service life and the maintenance you must do

The structural warranty is 10 years. The finishing warranty is 1 year, extendable to 2. Both run from the dates and on the terms stated in the signed quotation and handover documents, and both assume the maintenance below is actually done.

What sits outside warranty is stated rather than implied: unapproved alterations, poor foundations, site water entry, utility faults, corrosive exposure, missed seal or coating maintenance, misuse and relocation damage. The coating system is the part owners most often neglect. Cut edges, site-drilled penetrations and crevices need the approved touch-up, and a coastal or high-moisture site needs an upgraded system specified before production, not after the first monsoon.

Roof drainage is the second. Overflow paths, gutter access, AC line routes and every roof penetration are left inspectable and are tested before the ceiling is closed, which is only useful if somebody looks at them once a year. Luxury appearance does not conceal drainage on this build, and it should not be made to.
