Detailed information about Porta Cabin

Porta Cabin and Portable Cabin: One Product, Two Names

Porta cabin and portable cabin are two names for the same factory-built steel cabin. Construction buyers, contractors and government tenders in India mostly write porta cabin. Corporate and residential buyers often search portable cabin instead. The product underneath does not change. It is a welded mild-steel chassis with an insulated enclosure, a finished interior and protected electricals. We build it in a factory and deliver it to your site as one module. SAMAN therefore publishes one specification, one price ladder and one quotation process for both names, on this single page.

This matters for more than vocabulary. Some suppliers keep separate porta cabin and portable cabin pages with different claims. Buyers then compare two versions of the same product with mismatched numbers. Our approach is the opposite. Every figure on this page traces to one controlled costing and specification workbook. The downloadable PDF in the hero carries the same numbers, the version marker and the source workbook they came from.

How SAMAN Builds Every Porta Cabin

Every cabin starts as a welded chassis of 100x50x3 mm MS C-channel with cross stiffeners. The floor is what a cabin lives or dies by, so the chassis gets the heaviest steel. Onto that frame we fix an 18 mm Bison cement-fibre floor board. A 1.3 mm vinyl layer finishes the walking surface. The wall frame uses 50x50 mm MS square pipe with an approximately 60 mm cavity. Outside, the walls carry 1.2 mm specially corrugated MS sheet. Inside, they carry 8 mm pre-laminated MDF lining. The roof runs 1.4 mm corrugated MS sheet over its own stiffener grid.

Insulation is standard, not an upsell. Walls take 25 mm glass wool or 12 mm Heatlon. The roof takes 50 mm glass wool at 42 kg/m³ nominal density, because that is where Indian sun load actually hits. The 7x3 ft MS-framed main door opens outward. Two-track powder-coated aluminium sliding windows carry 4 mm glass. Electricals run in concealed or protected copper wiring on a 1.5/2.5/4 sq.mm schedule. A distribution board with MCB/RCCB protection and earthing controls the circuits. Ventilation is natural cross-flow, with fan, exhaust and AC provisions as scheduled. Plumbing is not included unless your approved scope states it.

Two build facts deserve honest emphasis. First, engineered lifting points are part of the structure, and the cabin is handled only by the approved lifting and support-point drawing. Approximate completed unit weight: 10x10 ft approximately 1.5 tonnes; 20x8 ft approximately 2.4 tonnes; 20x10 ft approximately 3.0 tonnes; 20x12 ft approximately 3.6 tonnes; 30x10 ft approximately 4.5 tonnes; and 40x10 ft approximately 6.0 tonnes. These are approximate completed unit weights, not floor load capacity, roof load capacity, lifting capacity or structural load capacity. Actual completed unit weight can vary with the approved configuration, fit-out and optional equipment. The cabin is designed to be moved, and that is what makes it genuinely portable rather than merely prefabricated. Second, the final colour, openings and fit-out follow your approved drawing. The images on this page are approved SAMAN visualisations of the standard build, not a fixed appearance. The complete line-by-line specification sits in the Specifications tab and in the versioned PDF.

What Buyers Use Porta Cabins For

Site and project offices are the largest use. A contractor orders the cabin with the work order, and the office lands before the first excavation. General-purpose rooms come next: security points, drivers' rooms, first-aid rooms, storage and sample rooms. Retail and service use covers shops, kiosks, ticket counters and reception units, usually in the shop configuration. Sanitation and project-support configurations round out the family, from toilet-fitted cabins to stores.

The common thread is time. Civil construction of a 200 sq ft room takes months and leaves debris. A porta cabin arrives finished, works the same day it is placed, and moves to the next site when the project ends. That reuse is also why buyers who once rented often switch to owning.

Porta Cabin on Terrace and Rooftop

Rooftop placement is one of the most asked questions on this page. It often works, because the module weighs far less than an equivalent masonry room. Two checks decide feasibility. First, your structural engineer must confirm the slab can carry the cabin at the planned position. Second, there must be crane or lifting access to the roof level.

Share the building height, slab details and access photos with your enquiry. We then advise on the lift plan and quote accordingly. However, never place a cabin on a roof without the engineer's confirmation. That check is the buyer's responsibility, and we state it plainly.

How to Compare Porta Cabin Quotations

Cheap quotes usually hide in the specification, not the price. Compare line items, and the differences surface fast. Ask every vendor for the same five numbers:

What does a porta cabin cost per square foot?

The published band runs from ₹1,187.50 to ₹1,437.50 per sq ft ex-GST, depending on size. The 20x10 ft cabin at 200 sq ft works out to ₹1,250 per sq ft. Compare the published price for each standard size in the table and request an itemised quotation for the configuration you need.

What sizes does a porta cabin come in?

Six published sizes: 10x10, 20x8, 20x10, 20x12, 30x10 and 40x10 ft, all 8.5 ft high. Send your requirement with your enquiry if none of the six matches your footprint.

Can a porta cabin be installed on a terrace or rooftop?

Rooftop or terrace placement depends on the building. Ask your structural engineer to confirm that the slab can carry the cabin at the planned position, and check that there is lifting access to roof level. Share the building height, slab details and access photos with your enquiry.

Do I need a foundation?

The base is set by the project engineering approved for your order, and any base work is outside the published price. Share your site conditions with your enquiry so the base requirement can be confirmed for your location.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 7–21 working days from confirmed order, plus transit. Your quotation confirms the schedule that applies to your order.

Is GST included in the published prices?

Prices are shown ex-GST with the 18% inclusive figure alongside, under HSN 9406 for prefabricated buildings.

How long does a porta cabin last?

Service life depends on use, siting and maintenance. Warranty period and exclusions are confirmed in the final quotation for your order.

Can I customise the layout?

Yes. Partitions, furniture, AC provision, colours and openings follow the drawing approved with your order. Send your requirement with your enquiry and it will be quoted against that drawing.
