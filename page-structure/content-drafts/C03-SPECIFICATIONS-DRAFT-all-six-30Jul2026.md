**STATUS: APPROVED.** Owner approval given by SAMAN in writing, 30 Jul 2026. Issued by Fable 5.

**Source:** `SAMAN_MASTER_64_Products_Detailed_Technical_Specs_9_Sizes_Report-with-price- PR.xlsx`, per-product sheets 28 to 32, `Detail` column. Group structure matches the live `/product/porta-cabins` Specifications tab: `Steel Structure` (8 rows), `Walls, Roof, Floor & Insulation` (10 rows), `Doors, Windows, Electrical & Services` (12 rows), 30 rows per product.

**One approved departure from the workbook.** Its `Warranty` row reads *"Warranty period and exclusions are confirmed only in the final quotation"*, which is vague. All six entries carry the §18 canonical sentence instead, verbatim:

> 5-year structural warranty and 1-year finishing warranty as standard; finishing warranty extendable to 2 years on request, confirmed at quotation.

Every other row is the workbook value verbatim. L4 applies: implement exactly, changing a word breaks acceptance.

**Reference size** is `20x10x8.5 ft` on five products and `10x10x8.5 ft` on `small-office-cabin`, matching its three-size ruling.

## portable-office

`product`: Portable Office Cabin · `cluster`: Portable Office · `referenceSize`: 20x10x8.5 ft

**fullTechnicalDescription**

> Factory-built MS portable office cabin supplied as one complete unit across nine standard sizes from 10x10 to 40x12 ft. The structural frame, cladding, insulation, flooring and electrical package are all fitted and tested before dispatch, so the cabin is placed, connected and occupied without site fabrication.

**commonPlatformSummary**

> Common platform: MS structural frame with insulated panel construction, factory-fitted electricals, and dispatch from Bengaluru or Greater Noida in 7 to 21 working days. Newly fabricated, not a converted shipping container.
