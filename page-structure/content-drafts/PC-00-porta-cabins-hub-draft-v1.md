**H2: Porta Cabin or Container Office: Which One Do You Need?**

Buyers often shortlist a porta cabin against a container office. The two are different structures, however, and the difference decides the price and the feel. A porta cabin is purpose-built on an MS C-channel chassis with a ~60 mm insulated wall cavity. It is lighter, arrives in more size options, and starts at a lower price point. A container office, in contrast, starts from a much heavier corten-steel shipping-container shell. That shell suits brutal handling, stacking and long outdoor service, and it carries a higher starting price.

Choose the porta cabin when you need an economical, well-insulated room fast, in a size matched to your plot. Consider a container office instead when the structure will be lifted repeatedly, stacked, or left in aggressive outdoor conditions for years. SAMAN manufactures both families in the same factories. If your enquiry fits the container side better, tell us the duty in your message. We will quote the right platform rather than force this one.
