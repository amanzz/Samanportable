# C-08: THE TWO HELD PAGES, RULED ON QUERY EVIDENCE — 02 Aug 2026

The PIB's stated reason for holding these two, an uncovered capsule and container-room family, is **not supported by the top-20 tables**. No capsule query appears on either page. `container room price` appears on `prefab-container-homes` itself, not on either held page. The hold was correct procedure; the stated cause was wrong, and the real picture is different for each page.

## PAGE A · /product/container-houses/prefabricated-container-house — KEEP AND NARROW, DO NOT REDIRECT

Evidence: 12 of 20 queries are shared with the prefab child, which is severe splitting. But it **outranks the child on the money queries**: `container house price in india` at 3.5 against the child's 5.7, `2 bhk container house price in india` at 3.3 against the child's 5.0, `3 bhk container house price in india` at 4.1, `1 bhk container house price in india` at 5.9, `buy container house in india` at 4.7.

Redirecting the better-positioned URL into the weaker one is precisely the mistake the C-01 law exists to prevent. Equally, leaving two duplicate product pages live guarantees permanent splitting.

**Ruling, per SOP section 4.3, narrow the weaker page's scope:** this page stops being a duplicate product page and becomes the **national container house price and BHK guide**, which is the intent it already ranks for. It keeps `container house price in india` and the 1, 2 and 3 BHK price family. It loses its product framing, its ladder, its Product schema and its Offer blocks, so it can never again compete as a product page. The prefab child keeps the module, colony and Bangalore intent it already owns.

**Not in this event.** No change to this page in PR #111. It stays a live 200 exactly as it is now. Fable 5 writes the repositioning copy as its own small event once C-08 publishes, so one page's rewrite never delays a five-page cluster.

## PAGE B · /product/container-houses/prefabricated-container-home — REDIRECT TO THE HUB

Evidence: this page owns nothing. Its earned clicks break down as roughly 66 of 117 on the hub's own term family (`container house` 50 clicks at position 5.0, `container home` 12, `container homes` 4), and the remainder on prefab term variants (`prefab container house` at 28.6, `prefabricated container` at 6.1, `container prefab` at 13.1, `prefab containers` at 12.7) that belong by name to the prefab child and where we rank poorly anyway.

A page whose best asset is another page's primary keyword has no keyword of its own. That is the M1 precedent applied to a home product.

**Ruling: 301 to `/product/container-houses`, single hop.** Two thirds of its equity is head-term equity and the hub is where it belongs; the prefab-variant remainder is small, badly positioned, and the child is the rightful owner. Route retirement follows the standard pattern, feed entry removed, every internal link repointed directly.

**Monitoring condition, binding:** record this page's 90-day clicks, impressions and top-20 queries as the before-state in the C-08 closure record. If `container house` head-term clicks on the hub have not absorbed this within 60 days, the redirect is reviewed rather than assumed correct.
