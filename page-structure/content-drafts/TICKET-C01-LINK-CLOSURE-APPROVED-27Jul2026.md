## 1 · THE FINDING THAT JUSTIFIES THIS EVENT

**66 live pages outside the product cluster relate to porta cabins, and together they earn 5,897 clicks.** The nine product pages earn roughly 2,300 between them.

**Your supporting content earns 2.5× what your money pages earn, and almost none of that authority flows into them.** That is the single largest untapped lever in this cluster. A page that ranks and links nowhere is authority sitting idle.

## 2 · THE RULE — one supporting page, one primary target

**Every supporting page links to exactly ONE cluster page.** No page links to two cluster pages, no page links to "the range" and then also a subpage. One page, one owner — the same discipline that governs the cluster itself.

Why: a blog linking to four cluster pages splits the signal four ways and tells Google nothing about which page owns the topic. One decisive link beats four hedged ones.

## 3 · PLACEMENT AND ANCHOR RULES

- **Contextual only.** The link sits inside a sentence that already exists in the body, on the words given. No link boxes, no "related products" modules, no footer blocks.
- **Never in the first paragraph.** That zone stays link-free.
- **Anchor text exactly as specified.** Do not extend, shorten or re-case it. All 66 anchors are unique site-wide — verified mechanically.
- **Zero bare exact-match anchors.** No link uses `porta cabin`, `porta cabins`, `portacabin` or `porta cabin price` as its whole anchor. Verified.
- **One link per source page.** If a source already links into the cluster, replace that link rather than adding a second.
- Where the specified anchor words do not appear naturally in the body, **STOP and report that page** — Fable 5 will supply a sentence. Do not invent one.
