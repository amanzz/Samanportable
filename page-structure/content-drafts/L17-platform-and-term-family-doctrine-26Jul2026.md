## 1. THE TRUE ARCHITECTURE

**Two platforms. Not 64 products.**

- **P1 FABRICATED** — newly fabricated MS sheet, MS pipe frame, aluminium sections. ~50% of orders customised.
- **P2 CONTAINER-DERIVED** — used shipping container, refurbished and rebuilt, supplied as new.

Every one of the 64 URLs resolves to **exactly one (Platform × Configuration) pair**, wearing the term its buyers actually search.

```
URL  =  PLATFORM  ×  CONFIGURATION  ×  TERM
         (2 real)     (real, ordered)   (search demand)
```

SAMAN's instruction to target every term is **strategically correct** — porta cabin, portable cabin, prefabricated cabin and modular cabin are different vocabularies belonging to different buyer segments, and conceding those terms concedes the market. It is only dangerous when two URLs wear different terms over the *same* configuration. That is the entire cannibalisation problem, and this doctrine closes it.

## 2. THE HONESTY MECHANISM — WHY 40% UNIQUE IS TRUE, NOT MANUFACTURED

Because roughly half of all orders are customised, **there is no single "the" specification.** Each page therefore publishes:

> **the as-built specification of the configuration that this page's buyers actually order, at the price stated for that configuration.**

That is a fact about real orders, not a marketing variation. It is also precisely what a buyer arriving on that page wants to see. Two consequences:

- Differentiation is achieved by **assigning a real configuration**, never by rewording a shared one. Synonym-swapping to hit a percentage remains an L15 fabrication defect.
- Every published value must already exist in the approved specification workbook. **Selecting an approved value is permitted. Inventing one is not.**

**Floor raised to 40%** (SAMAN, 26 Jul): ≥ 12 of the 30 rows must differ from the page's own hub. Ceiling stays 70%. Hubs exempt. The 6 HARD-COMMON rows stay byte-identical everywhere.

## 3. THE PLATFORM DISCLOSURE SENTENCE — mandatory, every page

One sentence, in the right-to-exist block, stating which platform the page describes. It is required for three reasons at once: it is true, it is the single strongest signal separating 64 near-identical pages for Google, and it converts — buyers care a great deal whether a unit is newly fabricated or container-derived.

- **P1 pages:** `Newly fabricated at our own works from MS sheet, MS pipe framing and aluminium sections — not a converted shipping container.`
- **P2 pages:** `Built from a shipping container shell, stripped, reinforced, insulated and finished at our works, and supplied as new.`
- **Dual pages** (Prefab Container Homes, Container Houses hub): both sentences, then the choice — `Both are offered; the comparison below shows which suits your site.`

## 4. P2 EARNS ITS UNIQUENESS AUTOMATICALLY

Container-derived units genuinely differ on the structural rows — there is no fabricated MS chassis, the corten shell is the structure, openings are cut and reinforced rather than framed, and the floor interfaces with the container's own cross-members. **P2 pages clear 40% on truth alone**, with no assignment needed: bottom frame, bottom stiffeners, floor frame, top frame, roof stiffeners, corner posts, exterior walls, roof and lifting provision all differ legitimately.

This resolves three previously-failing pages at a stroke: **Shipping Container Homes** (used containers, sold as new), **Container Houses** and **Luxury / Affordable Container Homes**.

**Prefab Container Homes** carries both platforms, so it becomes the cluster's genuine comparison page — the highest-value page in the cluster and the site's current #1 by clicks. It compares P1 and P2 honestly on cost, lead time, insulation, span and relocation. It stays L3-STRICT: I do not touch its URL, title, H1, meta or first 100 words.

## 5. THE FIVE CONFIGURATION AXES (the only legal sources of difference)

1. **Gauge / thickness ladder** — sheet and section thickness. Real, priced, customer-selected.
2. **Material grade** — lining, ceiling, floor finish, façade, doors, glazing. Real, priced.
3. **Fit-out and layout** — office, shop, toilet, dormitory, café, multi-zone. The `Layout / configuration` row plus electrical, ventilation and plumbing.
4. **Supply model** — **ready stock, fixed BOM, dispatched without a drawing** vs made-to-order. Confirmed real by SAMAN 26 Jul; it legitimately changes layout, finish, opening schedule and lead time.
5. **Platform** — P1 vs P2. The strongest axis, and the only one that is structural.

**THE PRICE-CONFIGURATION RULE:** price and configuration move together. A page cannot cost more and be specified identically, and it cannot be specified richer at the same price without saying why. Where the workbook gives a page a different price source, its specification follows that same source.

## 7. WHAT THIS FIXES THAT NOTHING ELSE COULD

Google's problem with this site was never the copy quality. It was that 64 URLs described what looked like one product. Naming the platform, publishing the real ordered configuration and stating on every page which sibling to choose instead gives every URL a defensible, truthful reason to exist. It also removes the risk that a customer receives a unit that does not match the page, because every page now describes a configuration SAMAN actually builds.
