## C · Conventions

Flat roof adds 4% of base. Extra height adds 6% of base per foot above 8.5 ft. Installation confirmed at quotation. Everything ex-GST, GST at 18% itemised separately, ex-GST shown as the primary figure.

## RULE

Any rate the calculator applies must appear in this card. Any option the calculator offers must have both a rate here and an approved specification behind it. An option with a rate but no specification is removed, not priced.
